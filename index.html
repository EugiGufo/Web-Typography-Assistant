What is the «Web Typography Assistant»? [⤣ hide]

Il «Tipografo per il web» è uno strumento online che semplifica e velocizza la preparazione dei testi per la pubblicazione sul web. Esso – lo strumento – sarà utile a tutti coloro che non sono indifferenti a come compaiono sugli schermi i testi da loro pubblicati, ma che non hanno abbastanza tempo o pazienza per il lungo lavoro manuale di correzione delle lineette (spesso confuse con i trattini), delle virgolette (spesso confuse con il simbolo dei pollici), degli apostrofi (spesso confusi con il simbolo del minuto), degli spazi unificatori (da utilizzare dopo gli articoli, le congiunzioni e le preposizioni e in tante altre situazioni), degli spazi doppi e degli spazi mancanti, e di tante altre cose noiose e ripetitive. Con un solo clic su un pulsante, qualsiasi persona – anche la più pigra o la meno esperta in tipografia – può rendere il testo pianificato per la pubblicazione molto più bello e leggibile.
The ‘Web Typography Assistant’ is an online tool that simplifies and speeds up the preparation of text for publication on the web. This tool will be useful to anyone who cares about how the text they publish appears on screen, but who lacks the time or patience for the laborious manual task of correcting en dashes (often confused with hyphens), quotation marks (often confused with the thumbs-up symbol), apostrophes (often confused with the minute symbol), non-breaking spaces (to be used after articles, conjunctions and prepositions, and in many other situations), double spaces and missing spaces, and many other tedious and repetitive tasks. With a single click of a button, anyone — even the laziest or least typographically savvy person — can make text intended for publication much more attractive and readable.
And since this version of my ‘Web Typographer’ is capable of following the typographical rules of various European languages and performing some additional operations for preparing text for the web, I recommend that new or occasional users consult the ‘Settings’ section before starting work.
Here, I list, using simple text, all the things my ‘Web Typographer’ can currently do:
— Multilingual typography support (Russian, English, Italian, French, Spanish, Portuguese and German).
— Language-specific handling of articles, prepositions, abbreviations and quotation marks.
— Automatic application of non-breaking spaces according to language conventions.
— Support for multiple quotation mark styles and HTML output formats.
— Removes unnecessary spaces before punctuation marks
— Inserts missing spaces after punctuation marks
— Removes spaces before percentage symbols
— Corrects spacing around round and square brackets
— Automatically inserts non-breaking spaces after conjunctions
— Automatically inserts non-breaking spaces after prepositions
— Binds articles to the following word (English, Italian, French and Spanish)
— Converts dimensions written with «x» into the multiplication symbol (×)
— Converts common fractions (¼, ½, ¾, ⅓, ⅔, ⅕, ⅖, ⅗, ⅘, ⅙, ⅚, ⅛, ⅜, ⅝, ⅞)
— Converts ®, ©, (TM) and (P) into their corresponding symbols
— Normalises apostrophes in Latin-script texts
— Automatically generates nested quotation marks
— Supports multiple quotation mark styles
— Supports multiple dash styles
— Converts URLs into clickable hyperlinks
— Converts bare domains into clickable hyperlinks
— Converts e-mail addresses into clickable mailto links
— Converts telephone numbers into clickable tel links
— Converts plain-text lists into HTML lists
— Creates non-breaking constructions for telephone numbers
— Binds years to dates
— Creates non-breaking date and month ranges
— Binds initials to surnames
— Formats measurement units and attaches them to numbers
— Handles BCE/CE abbreviations
— Handles abbreviations such as P.S., P.P.S., etc., e.g., i.e., and similar constructions
— Binds legal entity abbreviations to company names
— Formats references such as page numbers, figures and illustrations
— Formats addresses and common location abbreviations
— Inserts commas before coordinating conjunctions where required
— Handles language-specific hyphenation patterns and compound expressions
— Supports multilingual typographic rules
